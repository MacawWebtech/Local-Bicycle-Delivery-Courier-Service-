CONFIRM BEFORE COLLECTION

A few things to agree on.

Clear information prevents surprises at pickup and delivery.

Size & handling

Maximum parcel dimensions: 24 × 18 × 18 inches. Unusual shapes, fragile items and regulated goods need separate review.

Coverage & access

Confirm both addresses, any extended-area charges, stairs or entry restrictions before the operator accepts the job.

Changes & waiting

Agree on cancellation, waiting and return terms ahead of time. No charges or refund policy are implied here.

PRICING QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Clear details.
A better pickup plan.

Share your addresses, parcel information and preferred timing to request an accurate quote.

Planning a regular route?

Include the number of stops, collection frequency and typical parcel sizes. These details help review a suitable business arrangement.

Are these final prices?

No. These are sample planning prices. Your delivery operator must confirm the actual quote, taxes and applicable charges before acceptance.

Does express guarantee an arrival time?

No. Priority availability and the window depend on rider capacity, safe routing, weather and recipient access.

Are there discounts for recurring deliveries?

Recurring pricing can be discussed based on volume and route density. No discount or business plan is automatically applied.

Can I pay on this page?

No. Payment providers are not connected. A secure backend must create a verified quote and payment session before collecting payment.

What if the delivery needs a return trip?

Agree on the return process and pricing with your operator before pickup. The planning calculator does not include a return journey.
