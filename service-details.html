GOOD TO KNOW

Before your next delivery.

Questions about access, timing or a special parcel? Share the details before collection.

Can I choose an exact arrival time?

You can share a preferred window. A real operator must confirm feasibility; weather, route conditions and rider capacity can affect arrival.

What happens if a parcel is too large?

The operator should review dimensions and weight before accepting it. If it cannot be carried safely within the equipment limits, an alternative must be agreed.

Can you provide proof of delivery?

A connected backend can record a completion time, recipient confirmation and consented photo or signature. This page does not create delivery records.

How do returns or missed handoffs work?

Agree on a contact procedure, waiting policy and return arrangement before pickup. No real return policy or charge is implied by this page.
