PACKED FOR A SAFE JOURNEY

Small footprint.
Sensible parcel limits.

Pack securely, provide accurate dimensions, and share any access or handling needs. The courier must be able to ride safely with the load.

Ready before the rider arrives

Use a closed, sturdy package and keep loose parts secure. Label the recipient clearly and measure the packed parcel, including any outer wrapping.

Share stairs, entry codes and a contact number in the delivery notes. If a load needs extra care, ask for a review before arranging pickup.

Review cargo bike guidance

Suitable loads

Sealed documents, secure shop parcels and properly packed food. Cargo limit: 25 lb total, 24 × 18 × 18 in per parcel.

Keep these off the bike

No hazardous goods, weapons, illegal items, unsecured liquids or live animals. High-value, fragile or regulated items require operator review.

Safety sets the pace

Weather, road conditions, route access and rider capacity can affect availability. A sample window is never a delivery guarantee.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST PICKUP

A few useful answers.

Need help choosing a service? Share your parcel details and preferred window with the operator.

Prepare for a smoother pickup.

Choose the right window

Share when the order will be ready and when the recipient is available.

Keep details together

Have parcel dimensions, addresses and contact numbers ready before requesting collection.

How is the price calculated?

A real quote can account for distance, urgency, parcel size and delivery frequency. Try the homepage estimator for clearly labeled sample pricing.

Can I arrange several stops?

Discuss scheduled routes for multiple local stops. The operator should confirm the stop order, load capacity and timing before accepting a route.

Is tracking live?

No. The tracking example shows sample statuses only. Courier coordinates, ETA and proof of delivery need a connected backend and appropriate consent.

What if nobody is there to receive it?

Provide a reachable contact and clear instructions. A real operator should agree on a safe alternative handoff or return policy before collection.

How should I prepare a parcel?

Use a sealed, stable package and include its final dimensions and weight. Share fragile contents or handling needs before collection.

Can I choose a regular pickup slot?

Discuss scheduled collections for recurring needs. Confirm your opening hours, route stops and typical parcel volume before agreeing on a routine.

What happens in difficult weather?

Safety comes first. Road conditions and weather can affect availability or timing, so the operator should communicate any changes before departure.
