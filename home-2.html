A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first pickup.

Practical answers for a better local delivery routine.

A smoother first pickup

Have the parcel packed, the pickup window chosen, and the recipient’s contact details ready before requesting a courier.

  • Confirm size and weight.
  • Include access or handling notes.
  • Check that both addresses are covered.
Can I schedule recurring pickups?

Yes, that is the intended business service. A real operator must confirm your route, frequency, pickup windows, and capacity. Pickups must be confirmed by your delivery operator.

Is bicycle delivery always faster than a van?

No. Bicycles can be practical for dense urban routes, but timing depends on distance, traffic, weather, loading, and availability. We do not promise a fixed speed advantage.

What happens if an order is too large?

Use the sample cargo limits as a planning guide. The operator should review unusual loads before accepting them; an alternative transport provider may be required.

Can I see proof of delivery and invoices?

The planned business dashboard includes completion details, history, and billing views. These pages and a secure backend follow the homepage review; they are not connected here.

Is tracking live?

No. It shows manually selected stages. Live coordinates, dispatch, ETA updates, and notifications require a tracking API.
