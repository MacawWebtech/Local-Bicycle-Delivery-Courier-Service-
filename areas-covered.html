A FEW COVERAGE QUESTIONS

Clear boundaries.
Better planning.

A zone label is a starting point. A real operator should verify the route and requested service before booking.

Does a neighborhood match confirm my address?

No. This checker uses names only. It does not geocode addresses or test actual coverage polygons.

Can I deliver from a core area to an extended area?

That route may require advance planning. Confirm both addresses, rider capacity and the window with your real operator.

Is express available in every zone?

No express availability is claimed. Priority service depends on distance, safe routing, weather and courier capacity.

What if my neighborhood is not listed?

Contact your delivery operator to confirm coverage for your area.
