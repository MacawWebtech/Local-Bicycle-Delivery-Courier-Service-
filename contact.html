BEFORE YOU SEND

A few helpful answers.

Clear information helps a courier plan a safe, practical pickup.

Find your service
What details should a business inquiry include?

Share the pickup neighborhood, destination, approximate parcel size and weight, expected frequency and preferred pickup window. Do not include payment information.

Can I request an area outside the core zones?

Yes. Use Coverage request in the inquiry form and describe the route. A delivery operator must review the route and confirm availability before accepting a pickup.

Does sending a question book a courier?

No. An inquiry is separate from a delivery request. The request flow lets you review pickup and parcel information; dispatch needs a connected booking service.

What if my parcel needs special handling?

Describe its packaging and handling needs before requesting delivery. Restricted goods are not accepted, and fragile or unusually large loads need operator review.
